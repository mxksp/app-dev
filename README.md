# 🚖 Favorite Series: Taxi Driver (모범택시)


**Original Title**: 모범택시  
**Director**: Park Joon-woo (Season 1), Lee-dan (Season 2)  
**Release Year**: 2021  
**Genre**: Action, Crime, Thriller

---

## 🧭 Plot Summary

*Taxi Driver* follows Kim Do-gi, a former special forces officer whose life changes after his mother's murder. He becomes a driver for Rainbow Taxi Company, a secret organization that offers a "revenge-call" service to clients who have been wronged and helps them to exact vengeance.

> "If the law doesn't punish them, we will." – Kim Do-gi

---

## 👥 Main Characters

- **Kim Do-gi** – A former special forces officer turned vigilante taxi driver.
- **Jang Sung-chul** – Head of Rainbow Taxi Company.
- **Go Eun** – A skilled hacker and member of the Rainbow team.
- **Kang Ha-na** – A prosecutor investigating the secretive taxi service.
